Quick Form Rules

  • Always adjust incline while unloaded. Never change angle while standing on the board.
  • Keep core braced and movements slow and deliberate. No jumping or bouncing.
  • Stop immediately if experiencing sharp pain, dizziness, or tingling.

20° — Standard Mobility & Squat Pitch

The optimal general fitness incline. Elevates heels sufficiently to keep torso upright during deep squats, even with restricted ankle mobility.

Primary Muscle Recruitment:
Quadriceps VMO Ankle Dorsiflexors Achilles Tendon
Coaching Note: Ensure both boards are set to the identical angle setting before beginning double-leg squats. Step onto the center of each textured surface.

Foot Placement Guide

🦶 Heels Elevated: Targets quadriceps, isolates VMO, relieves ankle stiffness during squats.
🦶 Toes Elevated: Stretches gastrocnemius, soleus, Achilles tendon, and plantar fascia.
